## Counting and lesson matching

Vocabulary combines the existing pinyin, vocab, phrase and usage categories.
Only the vocabulary being tested assigns a lesson tag.
For a character-choice question, the correct character and its neighbouring text must form the taught word.
Words that merely appear in a sentence or distractor do not assign lesson tags.
A word taught in more than one lesson is eligible for those lessons.
Exam questions outside the taught vocabulary remain available for unrestricted practice, with empty lesson tags.
Distinct counts ignore source IDs and option order, and compare category, prompt and correct answer.
New questions use four choices and one correct answer, with mixed answer positions.
They cover familiar P3 contexts without picture prompts or unsupported response formats.
Original exam wording, choices, answers and source metadata are retained.

The [coverage record](vocabulary-coverage.yaml) lists the question IDs and target words for every lesson.
The [new-question review](authored-vocabulary.md) contains all newly authored questions and answers.

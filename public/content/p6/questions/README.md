## Scope and conventions

This follows the approved first-paper scope: language use and comprehension from Paper 2.
Paper 1 composition prompts and pictures appearing in some PDFs are outside this bank.
Category YAML files use the existing question-bank schema, and index.yaml is generated through the shared parser.
Source question numbers, PDF page references, answer-key pages and review flags are retained for checking against the scans.
Standalone IDs and passage sourceQuestion fields preserve printed numbering.
MCQ option order is retained, and correct stores the answer text.
Higher Chinese cloze accepts the printed option number or word.
Error correction retains the intentionally wrong character or word in its passage.
Comparison table rows become separate questions; paragraph summaries retain their stated 15- or 17-character limit.
Long answers are condensed reference answers based on the supplied keys; illustrative open answers are labelled.
Lesson tags are exact matches against P6 tingxie vocab[].word, with unmatched items left untagged.
Formatting and punctuation are normalized, while passage titles and target labels are editorial aids.
The transcribed contact details and websites are part of the examination text.

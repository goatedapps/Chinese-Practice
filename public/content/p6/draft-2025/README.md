# P6 2025 extracted question-bank draft

29 papers; 939 questions; 372 groups.
Chinese: 16 papers, 640 questions, 320 groups; 90 marks per paper.
Higher Chinese: 13 papers, 299 questions, 52 groups; 60 marks per paper.

[Review discrepancies and proposed corrections](REVIEW.md).

## Scope and conventions

This draft extracts Paper 2 questions, passages, option banks, marks and reference answers from every downloaded 2025 Chinese / Higher Chinese SA2 PDF.
Some source covers call the examination Preliminary; the download batch name is retained rather than rewriting the printed exam type.
Passage titles are editorial navigation labels where the source has no title.
Advertisements and schedules are transcribed as text or tables, retaining the information needed to answer their questions.
Source question numbers and PDF page references are preserved; duplicate question pages are extracted once.
Blank answer sheets, Paper 1 composition material and listening-only pages are outside this question-bank draft.
Chinese open answers consolidate marking points rather than reproduce every mark-allocation annotation verbatim.
Review notes identify answer conflicts, source typos and editorial supplements.
Your explicit review decisions have been applied.
Both requested reference answers (CH-StNicholas Q36 and HC-HenryPark Q22) have been approved and finalized.
Lesson tags use exact vocabulary matches and may be empty; they are provisional for later integration.

## Files

- `papers/`: one canonical JSON extraction per paper.
- `review/`: readable passages, questions, choices and answers for each paper.
- `questions/`: aggregated YAML categories and a draft-only index, validated with the app’s shared parser.
- `sources.json`: source PDFs, SHA-256 hashes, answer-key pages, counts and review notes.
- `validation.json`: structural and parser-validation results.

This folder preserves the reviewed extraction; all 939 questions have been merged into the app’s live `../questions/` bank alongside the 2026 content.
